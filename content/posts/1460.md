哈魯啊~今天給大家帶來一個美國Checking帳戶，開戶需要ITIN/SSN、護照以及地址證明

並且開戶最後一步需要付款5或10美元(失敗會退款)

開戶可能會SP一下TU信報，所以概率會出現開戶失敗

但是，這是目前唯一一個除Velo以外可以NRA在線開Checking的銀行！

作為一名職業雲居民，怎麼能放過他呢？

好了不說廢話了，進入正題~

**在線開戶教程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.affinityfcu.com/)，找到[Banking-Checking](https://www.affinityfcu.com/banking/checking/index.aspx)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-22-1024x394.png)

2.點擊Open an Account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-23-1024x457.png)

3.點擊Open a new membership

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-24-1024x706.png)

4.點擊Checking Account，選擇Affinity Cash Back Debit，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-25-1024x773.png)

5.填寫姓名、地址、國籍和護照信息

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-26.png)

6.填寫手機號碼、郵箱地址、職業信息，設置用戶名及密碼後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-27-1024x779.png)

7.依次選擇後點擊Continue(最後一項可選No，選Yes即捐款5美元)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-28-1024x722.png)

8.根據個人需要回答問題，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-29-1024x731.png)

9.直接點擊Confirm

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-30-1024x710.png)

10.選擇I am not a U.S. citizen or resident, therefore this is not applicable，然後點擊Accept

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-31-1024x743.png)

11.輸入0後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-32-1024x751.png)

12.選擇付款方式後點擊Submit Application

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-33-1024x760.png)

13.根據提示上傳護照及ITIN信紙掃描件

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-34.png)

14.坐等開戶即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-35-494x1024.png)

15.App部分支持中文的哦~

![這張圖片的 alt 屬性值為空，它的檔案名稱為 image-37-473x1024.png](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-37-473x1024.png)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-36.png)
