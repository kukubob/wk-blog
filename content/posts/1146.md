**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.greatnorthernrail.com/)，找到[The Key Smartcard界面](https://www.greatnorthernrail.com/tickets/the-key-smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-7-1024x415.png)

2.下拉找到Sign up now

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-8-1024x588.png)

3.根據提示填寫個人信息，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-9-1024x633.png)

4.填寫收件地址及出生日期，然後點擊Get the Key Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-10-1024x629.png)

5.直接點擊Creat an account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-11-1024x628.png)

6.下拉找到Register for keyGo，然後點擊Go

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-12.png)

7.填寫銀行卡信息，然後點擊Sign up to KeyGo

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-13.png)

8.直接點擊Accept and proceed

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-14.png)

9.至此卡片就完成購買啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-15-1024x419.png)

10.點擊[Key Smartcard status](https://ticket.greatnorthernrail.com/account/smartcard-apply)即可查看申請進度~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-16-1024x635.png)

11.記得去[Payment details](https://ticket.greatnorthernrail.com/account/payment)界面取消綁定卡哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-17.png)
