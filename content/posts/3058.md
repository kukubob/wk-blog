哈魯啊~今天給大家帶來一款美國電子錢包~開戶只需要護照即可無需SSN/ITIN哦~

開戶後會提供一個美國Checking帳戶，包含一個Account Number和一個Routing Number，還會給你一個美元幣種的英國帳戶，包含一個BIC和一個GB開頭的IBAN

但是他家目前還不能主動出金，只能透過金融卡消費的方式使用餘額，有一點陰間，但誰讓你沒有ITIN呢，哈哈哈

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/9cfb3402d118e.gif)

開戶默認只提供虛擬卡，實體卡可根據個人需要訂購(免費)

實體卡和虛擬卡BIN是418515，是一張Blue Ridge Bank, National Association發行的Visa Debit卡，跟Enzo是親兄弟，哈哈哈

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-49-1024x613.png)

查詢BIN訊息可前往https://checkbin.net/哦~

好了，本身也不是啥陽間東西，就不說太多了，上正文~

**網上申請過程**

1.打開[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tt/app/ellis-mobile-banking-card/id1587961973)/[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ellis.app&hl=en_US&gl=US)，下載Ellis App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-13-56-23-066_com.android.vending-450x1024.jpg)

2.打開App，點擊Get Started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-13-59-29-691_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

3.輸入Email，點擊I have a referral code，輸入2PJ5S1，點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-13-59-35-048_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

4.輸入Email收到的認證碼，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-04-33-110_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

5.輸入名(First Name)和姓(Last Name)，點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-05-06-479_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

6.設置密碼，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-05-23-894-edit_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

7.創建一個四位數字的PIN

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-05-49-912_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

8.啟用生物識別認證(人臉/指紋)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-05-56-498_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

9.點擊MONEY方塊下的Get Started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-06-15-251_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

10.輸入手機號碼，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-07-39-011_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

11.輸入收到的簡訊認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-07-56-220_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

12.輸入出生日期，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-08-16-694_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

13.輸入護照號碼，或者點擊I have a Social Security Number輸入SSN號碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-08-23-714_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

14.選擇自己的國籍，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-09-22-484_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

15.點擊I am not a student即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-09-41-402_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

16.輸入收件地址，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-09-46-885_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

17.確認個人訊息，點擊Submit Application

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-10-10-022_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

18.點擊Upload your passport和Verify your address分別上傳護照和地址證明即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-22-14-10-20-353_com.ellis_.app_-450x1024.jpg)

19.一般一個工作日內即可通過審核

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-32.png)

20.審核通過後即可進入App看到卡片訊息，如需訂購實體卡點擊Get Card即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/73435706d51e55c1813e7a027afdd94-450x1024.jpg)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/037e89149f9d1a32c59b0297c702d3d-450x1024.jpg)

最後的最後，還是向大家募集一波資金，，愛你呦~
