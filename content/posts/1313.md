**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.tpexpress.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.tpexpress.co.uk/train-tickets/season-tickets/smart-tickets)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-95-1024x463.png)

2.點擊REGISTER FOR A SMARTCARD

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-96-1024x577.png)

3.點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-97-1024x491.png)

4.輸入個人信息，然後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-98.png)

5.點擊Please select an option，然後選擇Apply for a smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-99-1024x710.png)

6.輸入生日，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-100.png)

7.輸入收件地址，然後點擊右側的Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-101.png)

8.至此卡片就訂購完成啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-102.png)

9.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-103.png)
