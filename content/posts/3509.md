**網上申請過程**

1.打開[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/venmo/id351727428)/[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.venmo&hl=en_US&gl=US)，下載Venmo App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/1-473x1024.png)

2.打開Venmo App，點擊Create account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2.1-473x1024.png)

3.選擇Personal，然後點擊Get started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2.2-473x1024.png)

4.輸入手機號碼，然後點擊Send code

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/3.1-473x1024.png)

5.輸入收到的簡訊認證碼，然後點擊Confirm it

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/3.2-473x1024.png)

6.輸入個人訊息(名、姓、郵箱以及設置一個密碼)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/4.1-473x1024.png)

7.直接點擊Agree

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/5-473x1024.png)

8.等待加載即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/6.1-473x1024.png)

9.點擊Skip for now

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/6.2-473x1024.png)

10.添加一張美國發行的Debit Card，或者也可直接點擊Not now

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/7.1-473x1024.png)

11.選擇一個你想要的隱私設置

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/7.2-473x1024.png)

12.此時賬戶就註冊完成啦~點擊Allow notifications

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/8.1-473x1024.png)

13.點擊首頁最下面的Cards，然後點擊Venmo Debit Card: Now in metal

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/9.1-473x1024.png)

14.點擊Get the Venmo Debit Card

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/9.2-473x1024.png)

15.Debit Card提供如下圖設計，其中Onyx Metal和Rose Gold Metal是金屬卡~需要付費的，但是部分人看到的價格是US$5.00，部分人看到的是US$10.00

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/10.1-473x1024.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/10.2-473x1024.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/10.3-473x1024.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/10.4-473x1024.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/10.5-473x1024.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/11.1-473x1024.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/11.2-473x1024.png)

16.填寫收貨地址，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/12-473x1024.png)

17.輸入生日(月-日-年)和SSN/ITIN后四位，然後點擊Agree & continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/13-473x1024.png)

18.確認訂單信息，然後點擊Go to checkout

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14.1-473x1024.png)

19.使用綁定的支付方式完成付款

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14.2-473x1024.png)

20.上傳一份SSN卡/ITIN信和一份護照(外國也可以)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/15-473x1024.png)

21.上傳完成後等待審核即可~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/16-473x1024.png)

22.在等待審核期間，你可以輸入我的邀請碼，具體路徑在Me-右上角設置-下拉找到Have a code?，然後輸入邀請碼**U3z6pilkIwb**，只需要在輸入邀請碼後14天內通過Venmo發送US$10.00及以上付款，你我各得US$10.00

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-22-473x1024.png)
