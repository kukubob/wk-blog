**提供的地址**

土耳其

**支持的儲值方式**

銀行卡(Visa、Mastercard、American Express、Diners Club、Discover)

**運費**

以中國大陸和中國台灣為例，發送1kg文件的價格如下：

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-6-1024x470.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-7-1024x465.png)

綜上：發廉價DHL即可

**註冊流程**

1.打開[官網](https://fastarz.com/)，點擊[MY ACCOUNT](https://fastarz.com/usr/login)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-1024x429.png)

2.點擊[Don't Have An Account](https://fastarz.com/usr/register)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-1.png)

3.輸入名、姓、郵箱以及設置密碼，然後點擊SIGN UP

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-2.png)

4.選擇國家、生日，輸入手機號，選擇姓名以及偏好語言，點擊SIGN UP

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-3.png)

5.至此帳戶就註冊完成了~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-4-1024x814.png)

6.在[這個界面](https://fastarz.com/usr/YourTurkishAddressList)可以看到你的土耳其地址

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/image-5-1024x565.png)
