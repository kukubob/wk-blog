**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/tickets-and-savings/smartcards)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-30-1024x447.png)

2.下拉找到[Creat an account](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/train-tickets/register)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-31-1024x512.png)

3.填寫個人信息，然後點擊Creat

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-32-1024x556.png)

4.點擊Submit

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-33-1024x649.png)

5.進入[My payment界面](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/train-tickets/account-my-payments)，點擊Order a Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-34-1024x455.png)

6.填寫個人信息，然後點擊Order

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-35-1024x632.png)

7.至此你的卡片就訂購成功啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-36.png)
