開戶需要有英國信用記錄，最好一年以上~

Current帳戶默認提供一張卡，BIN是465865，是一張Barclays Bank UK發行的Visa Debit Card

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-26-1024x608.png)

Savings帳戶開戶時可以選擇要一張Cash Card，實體卡BIN是492826，是一張Barclays Bank UK發行的Visa Debit Card

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-27-1024x614.png)

巴克萊的Debit卡時可以定制的！如果你想要圖片或者自己上傳照片，每次需要10英鎊的發卡費，如果你選擇純色卡那就是免費滴~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-28-1024x686.png)

**網上開戶過程**

1.打開[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/barclays/id536248734)/[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.barclays.android.barclaysmobilebanking&hl=en_GB&gl=US)，搜索下載Barclays App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/000d9830b8a309fb8a89d0088b7f2fc-450x1024.jpg)

2.打開I'm new to Barclays

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-203638_Barclays-461x1024.png)

3.點擊Apply for an account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-203646_Barclays-461x1024.png)

4.選擇Personal account，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-203652_Barclays-461x1024.png)

5.點擊Choose an account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-203706_Barclays-461x1024.png)

6.選擇Everyday Banking，點擊Apply

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-204745_Barclays-461x1024.png)

7.直接點擊OK, get started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-204752_Barclays-461x1024.png)

8.直接點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-204759_Barclays-461x1024.png)

9.設置一個密碼，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205349_Barclays-461x1024.png)

10.直接點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205415_Barclays-461x1024.png)

11.輸入你的英國手機號

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205448_Barclays-461x1024.png)

12.選擇是否開啟通知

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205512_Barclays-461x1024.png)

13.直接點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205518_Barclays-461x1024.png)

14.直接點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205529_Barclays-461x1024.png)

15.點擊Checks we carry out

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205601_Barclays-461x1024.png)

16.選擇護照(Passport)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-205613_Barclays-461x1024.png)

17.輸入名、姓和生日，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-210458_Barclays-461x1024.png)

18.根據提示完成拍攝護照及活體識別

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-210505_Barclays-461x1024.png)

19.根據個人情況選擇合適的答案，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-210958_Barclays-461x1024.png)

20.選擇國籍、出生國家，輸入出生城市

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-211101_Barclays-461x1024.png)

21.輸入近三年地址信息

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Screenshot_20230120-211219_Barclays-461x1024.png)
