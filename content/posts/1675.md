**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://ecardinc.com/)，填寫Email，然後點擊APPLY NOW

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-1024x525.png)

2.填寫個人信息，然後點擊CONTINUE

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-1-1024x550.png)

3.填寫SSN/ITIN(無論是否填寫SSN/ITIN)，都需要上傳護照及地址證明

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-2-1024x495.png)

4.如果沒有ITIN/SSN也是支持開戶的

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-3-1024x477.png)

5.輸入手機號，填入簡訊認證碼，然後點擊CONTINUE

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-4-1024x717.png)

6.至此就申請完成啦~網頁提示需要24~48小時來處理申請

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-5-1024x509.png)

7.申請被批准後會有一封郵件哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/photo_2022-03-23_00-22-36-1024x470.jpg)

8.收到卡後打開啟用(激活)頁面，輸入卡號後點擊Activate/Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-72.png)

9.輸入有效期、郵編以及出生日期

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-66-1024x710.png)

10.點擊Creat User

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-68-1024x437.png)

11.輸入CVV及有效期，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-69-1024x665.png)

12.建立一個User ID及密碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-70-1024x712.png)

13.至此卡片就啟用(激活)成功啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-71-1024x261.png)

實體卡長這樣~
