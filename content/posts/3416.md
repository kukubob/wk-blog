今天的文章是一個熱心的讀者提供的DP，如果你也想提供DP，請聯繫郵箱admin@wkbhjlq.tw

首先呢，這是一個電子錢包，不是銀行，其次就是開通只需要護照和人臉識別，至於查不查信用...改日再議(已確認會SP Equifax)，總體來說還是比較簡單的

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/image-5-1024x396.png)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/image-4.png)

開戶默認提供一個Account Number(英國)、一個Sort Code(英國)，默認沒有卡，可以自己選擇要實體卡還是虛擬卡(都免費)

Sort Code為040085(英國)

實體卡和虛擬卡BIN是537367，是一張Transact Payments發行的Mastercard Debit卡

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/image-3-1024x611.png)

查詢BIN訊息可前往https://checkbin.net/哦~

****網上**申請過程**

1.打開App Store/Google Play，搜索下載Algbra App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-35-10-154_com.android.ve_-450x1024.jpg)

2.下載後打開App，點擊Join the movement

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-39-33-038_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

3.輸入Email，點擊Agree and continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-39-36-943_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

4.打開郵箱點擊驗證

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-39-56-263-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

5.輸入手機號碼，點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-40-19-542_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

6.輸入收到的簡訊認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-40-36-602-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

7.直接點擊Allow notifications

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-41-26-098_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

8.輸入法定名(Legal first name)、姓(Legal last name)以及生日(Date of birth)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-41-51-252_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

9.輸入郵編查找地址

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-43-13-509-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

10.確認信息後點擊Confirm

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-44-25-533-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

11.選擇Passport進行身份驗證

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-44-46-505-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

12.驗證完成後等待一會，一般一分鐘內即可看到結果

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-48-57-892_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

13.批准的頁面長這樣，點擊Set passcode

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-53-38-706-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

14.設置一個6位數的密碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-53-44-963_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

15.設置完成後會彈出這個頁面，點擊Order now即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-54-04-609_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

16.選擇你要的卡種類(虛擬卡or實體卡)，這裡以實體卡為例

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-54-11-420_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

17.選擇喜歡的顏色，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-54-32-046_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

18.選擇一個想要印在卡上的名字

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-54-53-806-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

19.確認郵寄地址

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-55-13-311-edit_com.algbr_-450x1024.jpg)

20.設置卡片PIN

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-55-23-428_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

21.至此卡片就訂購成功啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-55-39-902_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

22.虛擬卡同理哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Screenshot_2022-12-07-20-56-35-949_com.algbra.Ban_-450x1024.jpg)

最後的最後，還是放上一個，掰掰~
