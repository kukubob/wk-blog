**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.lner.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.lner.co.uk/buy-tickets/season-tickets/)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-76-1024x540.png)

2.下拉找到Get your Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-77-1024x465.png)

3.在右側填寫個人信息，然後點擊Create an account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-78.png)

4.在新界面直接下拉找到Sing up for a new Smartcard，填寫地址，上傳照片後點擊Submit

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-79.png)

5.至此卡片就訂購成功啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-80-1024x390.png)

6.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-81.png)
