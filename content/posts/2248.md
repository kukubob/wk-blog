首先這是一個電子錢包，發行的也是預付卡，BIN是498597

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-35-1024x611.png)

BIN可在BIN Checker網站或Telegram Bot查詢哦~

開卡需要實名製，一個用戶可以開通7張虛擬卡，實體卡只能去線下購買後綁定

儲值支持銀行轉賬以及卡片直接儲值(目測N26可成功支付)

目前測試支持土耳其區App Store但是不支持Apple Pay以及Google Pay

基本信息大概就是這些了，接下來進入正題吧~

**網上申請過程**

1.打開[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/ozan-superapp/id1120101494)/[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ozan.android)，搜索Ozan SuperApp

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-12-473x1024.png)

2.打開App一頓亂點(實在打不出那個字母)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-13-473x1024.png)

3.選擇國家，輸入手機號並完成認證

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-14-473x1024.png)

4.點擊Evet, davet kondum var，輸入邀請碼ZG69391297

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-15-473x1024.png)

5.輸入Email並完成認證

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-16-473x1024.png)

6.輸入名(Ad)和姓(Soyad)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-17-473x1024.png)

7.點擊Onayla

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-18-473x1024.png)

8.設置一個6位數字的PIN

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-19-473x1024.png)

9.此時帳戶就創建成功啦~接下來點擊下方的SuperCard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-20-473x1024.png)

10.點擊Sanal SuperCard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-21-473x1024.png)

11.點擊Doğrula

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-22-473x1024.png)

12.選擇你的國籍

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-23-473x1024.png)

13.輸入名、姓和出生日期(日/月/年)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-24-473x1024.png)

14.輸入兩次Passport號碼以及身份證號碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-25-473x1024.png)

15.輸入地址(可非土耳其地址)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-26-473x1024.png)

16.選擇你的職業

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-27-473x1024.png)

17.點擊Devam

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-28-473x1024.png)

18.點擊Devam，完成拍攝護照

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-29-473x1024.png)

19.完成自拍

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-30-473x1024.png)

20.點擊Devam

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-31-473x1024.png)

21.完成一段視頻錄製

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-32-473x1024.png)

22.用英語簽字，然後點擊Devam

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-33-473x1024.png)

23.至此身份認證就完成啦~等待審核通過即可開始發行卡片啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-34-473x1024.png)

24.身份認證成功後會有通知喔~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-36-473x1024.png)

25.發行卡片的界面長這樣~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/photo_2022-05-09_17-20-07-473x1024.jpg)
