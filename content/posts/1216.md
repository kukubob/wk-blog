話不多說上乾貨~

**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.eastmidlandsrailway.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.eastmidlandsrailway.co.uk/tickets-discounts/select-a-delivery-option/smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-43-1024x522.png)

2.下拉找到[Order your Smartcard](https://www.buytickets.eastmidlandsrailway.co.uk/smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-44.png)

3.切換到Register，填寫姓名郵箱，設置密碼後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-45.png)

4.填寫地址信息，然後點擊Order Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-46.png)

5.至此卡片就訂購成功啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-47.png)

6.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-48.png)
