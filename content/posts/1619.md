今天介紹Equifax的信報查詢~

**註冊過程**

1.打開[Equifax官網](https://www.equifax.com/)，點擊右上角的Log In

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-55-1024x445.png)

2.點擊Register for Free

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-56.png)

3.根據提示填寫姓名、生日、ITIN/SSN等信息

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-57.png)

4.填寫你的地址，然後點擊CONTINUE

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-58.png)

5.如果信用記錄足夠長，此時就可以直接跳到這個頁面~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/0484547a80832df3b597d8d4857b63f-1024x527.png)

6.首頁可以看到分數哦~注意該分數是VantageScore 3.0模型的分數~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image-28-1024x406.png)
