**提供的地址**

紐西蘭

**支持的儲值方式**

信用卡/金融卡(Visa、Mastercard或American Express)

**賬戶類型**

賬戶分為個人賬戶和商業帳戶

個人賬戶分為Personal、Personal(yearly)和Premium

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-30-1024x428.png)

商業帳戶分為Business、Premium和Virtual Office

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-29-1024x422.png)

**註冊流程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.privatebox.co.nz/)，點擊[Personal Use](https://www.privatebox.co.nz/personal/)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-18-1024x432.png)

2.點擊Try for just $1

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-19-1024x401.png)

3.根據需要選擇不同的計劃，然後點擊Let's get started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-20-1024x434.png)

4.選擇地址(兩個街道地址，七個郵政信箱)，輸入名(First Name)和姓(Last Name)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-21-1024x580.png)

5.輸入個人信息(Email、電話號碼和居住地址)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-22.png)

6.設置密碼，輸入www.wkbhjlq.tw

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-23.png)

7.輸入信用卡/金融卡訊息，預授權1.00紐西蘭元(Visa、Mastercard或American Express)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-24-1024x630.png)

8.預授權完成後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-25-1024x551.png)

9.上傳證件和地址證明，選擇出生國家

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-26.png)

10.輸入開通帳戶原因，點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-27.png)

11.錄製一段視頻上傳即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-28-1024x479.png)
