**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://vitracash.com?ref_id=59847144)，點擊Request Access

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-60-1024x517.png)

2.輸入郵箱，然後點擊Get Invite

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-61.png)

3.打開[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vitra.android&pcampaignid=pcampaignidMKT-Other-global-all-co-prtnr-py-PartBadge-Mar2515-1)/[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/vitra/id1575122090?l=en)下載Vitra App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/41f68a30786fbdf906c56993da34cb2-768x1024.png)

4.點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/4ac66f670af4df08bd247a4602292b7-577x1024.jpg)

5.輸入手機號，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/5bab201a4eb61bce326bbbc5bbf28ef-577x1024.jpg)

6.輸入簡訊認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/微信图片_20211228000740-575x1024.jpg)

7.輸入姓(Last Name)、名(First Name)、郵箱以及生日，設置密碼後點擊Sign up

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/4aac54655d965b1436d40d0683ad495-578x1024.jpg)

8.選擇英國(United Kingdom)後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/5b608bae57fbc31fa0c48ac9a2eb92e-577x1024.jpg)

9.點擊Start

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/7623ff12ef47ee42d6b8b613ddc956f-612x1024.jpg)

10.勾選協議後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/1c5bee7abc9dfc99202d3139a53fa3e-614x1024.jpg)

11.點擊Continue後根據提示完成自拍

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/b031acf50c0052b18f263b32e963d6e-613x1024.jpg)

12.選擇護照發行國後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/4fa61f41e872a04922a35b514ec27c3-613x1024.jpg)

13.直接點擊Continue，根據提示拍攝護照

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/fc97437f2707baad446f4c5e977883e-613x1024.jpg)

14.直接點擊Confirm

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/e4289f39e5cb123e94bf38bedc53a01-614x1024.jpg)

15.點擊Finish等待審核即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/8a4d4c844914ada5ac74c09ee7fbd53-607x1024.jpg)

16.這時回到App已經可以看到卡號等信息了

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/微信图片_20211228002522-578x1024.jpg)
