**網上購買過程**

1.打開[官網](https://esim.5ber.com/)，點擊[Get it Now](https://esim.5ber.com/order)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-1024x659.png)

2.選擇數量，然後點擊CHECKOUT

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-1-1024x413.png)

3.點擊Skip

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-2-1024x569.png)

4.根據提示創建一個賬戶

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-3-1024x393.png)

5.輸入郵寄地址，選擇合適的付款方式付款即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-4-1024x656.png)

6.如果您是中國大陸地址，可以點擊圖標找客服備註一下中文地址哦~中國大陸順豐包郵

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-5.png)
