**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.crosscountrytrains.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.crosscountrytrains.co.uk/tickets/smart)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-37-1024x653.png)

2.點擊[new Smartcard here](https://www.buytickets.crosscountrytrains.co.uk/smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-38-1024x580.png)

3.切換到Register，填寫姓名郵箱，設置密碼，然後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-39.png)

4.填寫地址，然後點擊Order Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-40.png)

5.至此卡片就訂購完成啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-41.png)

6.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-42.png)
