**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://buy.chilternrailways.co.uk/)，點擊右上角的Sign in

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-1024x527.png)

2.切換到Register，填寫個人信息，然後點擊Register now

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-1-1024x601.png)

3.之後進入郵箱找到激活郵件，點擊Validate account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-2.png)

4.驗證完成後進入[My Smartcards](https://buy.chilternrailways.co.uk/account/details/smartcards)界面，點擊Request a new card

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-3-1024x588.png)

5.根據提示填寫你的個人信息及收貨地址，點擊Submit即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-4-1024x860.png)

6.至此卡片就完成訂購啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-5-1024x460.png)

7.郵箱也會收到一封確認信喔~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-6.png)
