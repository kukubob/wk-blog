哈魯啊~擼了這麼多的鐵路智慧卡，有小夥伴私信我問我：這些都沒照片的沒意思啊~

那麼今天介紹一款帶照片的鐵路智慧卡，沒錯就是標題說的Scotrail

首先呢這是一個鐵路公司(廢話)，其次呢...

沒有其次，接下來就是正文了~

**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.scotrail.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.scotrail.co.uk/smartcard/get-your-free-smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-49-1024x536.png)

2.點擊Get your free Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-50-1024x505.png)

3.點擊Creat new account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-51-1024x509.png)

4.填寫姓名及地址

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-52.png)

5.填寫生日、電話號碼、郵箱地址，設置密碼后點擊Preview

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-53.png)

6.直接點擊Creat account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-54.png)

7.點擊Order Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-55.png)

8.根據提示上傳照片，然後點擊Checkout

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-56.png)

9.直接點擊Proceed to comfirmation page

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-57.png)

10.直接點擊Order now

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-58.png)

11.至此卡片就完成購買啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-59.png)

12.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-60-1024x281.png)
