**網上申請過程**

1.找一部正經人用的手機，打開[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/enzo-wealth/id1583834324)/[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.enzowealth.platform&hl=en_US&gl=US)，搜索下載Enzo Wealth

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/aa6394ca452077952ffb403640f0743-450x1024.jpg)

2.打開App，點擊Get Started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/7b328a564535f90b0b40f8264f35de6-450x1024.jpg)

3.輸入手機號碼並接碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/4990abfd9f7e6085c503a95d7fdb4df0_-473x1024.jpg)

4.輸入Email並接碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/26844593c67dec9866783dc652008d45_-473x1024.jpg)

5.輸入名(First Name)和姓(Last Name)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/0fb129322bc6d39be2a80879723f33e6_-473x1024.jpg)

6.輸入出生日期(月-日-年)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/6a9cffd13178f376cf9ec280ff25b488_-473x1024.jpg)

7.輸入居住地址(美國)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/2af1470041fe5ca4c976f4bfc9c34bdb_-473x1024.jpg)

8.輸入SSN/ITIN即可完成開戶

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/7ba316bf7508801c0643e0a8aafc96a3_-473x1024.jpg)
