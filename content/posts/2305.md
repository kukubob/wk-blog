今天給大家帶來一款英國銀行帳戶，沒錯就是Chase UK~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image.jpeg)

開戶默認提供一張虛擬卡，實體卡可根據需要訂購，免費哦~

申請會Soft Pull Transunion，建議一年以上信用記錄嘗試，當然，你證件夠硬(UK護照等)隨便哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/WeChat-圖片_20221025191323-450x1024.jpg)

卡片BIN為51676785，是一張J P Morgan Europe發行的萬事達金融卡

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-49-1024x615.png)

實體卡長這樣哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-50.png)

*現在Chase UK搞活動，填寫邀請碼開戶你我各得20英鎊！邀請碼eG2QaOxJ，開戶記得填寫哦~

**網上申請過程**

1.打開[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chase.intl)/[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/GB/app/id1517121245?mt=8&c=mkt-469_uk_gl_bau_sea_gg_acq_xdv_pr_adp_at1_brand_pure&af_ad=pure&pid=google&af_js_web=true&af_channel=paid-search&web_prospect_id=863dd1a4-7755-477c-9fe2-046abd608a71&af_keywords=brand)，下載Chase UK App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-37-473x1024.png)

2.打開[連結](https://link.go.chase/emNK/refer?code=eG2QaOxJ)，自動跳轉到App，點擊Join Chase

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-38-473x1024.png)

3.點擊I'm ready

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-39-473x1024.png)

4.輸入First Name和Last Name，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-40-473x1024.png)

5.輸入出生日期(日/月/年)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/photo_2022-05-13_00-52-38-473x1024.jpg)

6.輸入英國流動電話號碼，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-41-473x1024.png)

7.輸入收到的簡訊認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/photo_2022-05-13_01-14-15-473x1024.jpg)

8.輸入郵箱地址，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-42-473x1024.png)

9.設置一個6位數字的密碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-43-473x1024.png)

10.選擇自己的國籍，然後點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-44-473x1024.png)

11.輸入家庭地址(英國)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-45-473x1024.png)

12.選擇Yes，點擊Next

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-46-473x1024.png)

13.選擇合適的證件，然後點擊Next完成拍照

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-47-473x1024.png)

14.拍一張自拍

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-48-473x1024.png)

接下來跟著流程走就可以了，由於我已經開戶成功了無法進行到下一步，只有一點需要注意，到選擇稅務居民的時候一定要僅選擇UK，不論你是否為其他國家稅務居民~
