**網上申請過程**

1.打開App Store/Google Play，搜索下載ekko app

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/3C55B72003E392BA06EC76B8C57E337D-450x1024.jpg)

2.打開App，點擊Create Account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/46DC0079F92922E900294E7D66695305-450x1024.jpg)

3.填寫邀請碼**R5TXR**，點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/D4C2A1085FEED15D349F8585F7D5584A-450x1024.jpg)

4.選擇Yes

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/579AE8D212C5835F8C63B5A35ED85587-450x1024.jpg)

5.輸入名(First name)和姓(Surname)，點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/57E595D24FA786EA2857ED04D0781110-450x1024.jpg)

6.輸入Email，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/52A813110D8A8B0080547DB92D97C31C-450x1024.jpg)

7.設置一個6位數字的密碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/8ABFCD2AFE2EBF7B02BF3AC28069266C-450x1024.jpg)

8.去郵箱點擊Verify Email

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/QQ图片20220823002101-450x1024.jpg)

9.輸入手機號碼，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/0DEEDEC1B2343F70F81E7FA656770937-450x1024.jpg)

10.輸入收到的認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/QQ图片20220823002318-450x1024.jpg)

11.設置生物識別登入

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/77071705D29DDF2DB5D1976772C7CD7E-450x1024.jpg)

12.輸入出生日期，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/EC3BFEF0AB1A14870167F69F2F4B960F-450x1024.jpg)

13.選擇你的國籍，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/A1C0D7E3FED264BAEE4056CBE9044E02-450x1024.jpg)

14.輸入你的英國地址

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/B86D0F9FE930FBD85D3785E50801A8EF-450x1024.jpg)

15.選擇Passport，當然你有別的也可以

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/BF27A47E28CA656EDA753EE97067C2D3-450x1024.jpg)

16.拍攝護照

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/B67092A007DE62B25B01EB3AF5C66CFF-450x1024.jpg)

17.自拍一張美美噠

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/3FD99BBCE3323ED36C40BEBF5B349C26-450x1024.jpg)

18.選擇你的計劃(填寫邀請碼後前三個月免費)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/437B35FADBF250D3EAF6ED02BF0309FB-450x1024.jpg)

19.至此就開通成功啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/4AF6134A7E4835EFE1992F76BDEFA82A-450x1024.jpg)
