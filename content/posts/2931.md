早啊大家~今天給大家帶來一個HSBC新出的帳戶，Emm，其實出了不久了，我今天才知道而已

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image.png)

這個帳戶比較特別，需要是現有HSBC客戶才可以，且你需要有US、UK、AE或者Expat任意一國帳戶才可以，各國此帳戶介紹頁如下：

United States(美國): https://www.us.hsbc.com/checking-accounts/products/global-money/

United Kingdom(英國): https://www.hsbc.co.uk/international/travel-money-card/

United Arab Emirates(阿聯): https://www.hsbc.ae/current-accounts/products/global-money/

Expat: https://www.expat.hsbc.com/accounts/products/global-money/

由於阿聯賬戶難度過大，本文就放過他吧~著重講講Expat和UK帳戶的區別

首先體現在卡上面，兩者都是英國發行的Visa Debit卡，UK發行的BIN為459681，Expat發行的BIN為459682，兩者均不收取FTF和跨境ATM取現手續費

兩者均支持Apple Pay/Google Pay，也算是破了Expat不支持Apple Pay的遺憾

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-1-1024x613.png)

查詢BIN訊息可前往www.checkbin.net

其次就是賬戶上面有些許區別，具體體現在UK會給你一個單獨的Current Account Number和Sort Code，而Expat沒有這個服務，除此之外Expat多了個THB帳戶，再就沒有區別了，哦還有一個，使用App不一樣~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/b80a15d0aa914.gif)

**網上申請過程**(以HSBC Expat為例)

1.打開HSBC Expat App，點擊下面的Multiccurrency Bank Accounts(UK用戶路徑為International Service-Global Money)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/1-461x1024.jpg)

2.選擇Gloabl Money

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Screenshot_2022-10-19-14-08-37-124_com.hsbc_.expat_.hsbcexpat-461x1024.jpg)

3.點擊Get started

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/3-461x1024.jpg)

4.勾選協議，點擊Sign up

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/4-461x1024.jpg)

5.點擊Remind me later

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/5-461x1024.jpg)

6.至此這個賬戶就成功開通了~現在去訂購卡，先點擊Back to my account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/6-461x1024.jpg)

7.回到主頁後點擊新開通的Global Money Account

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/7-461x1024.jpg)

8.點擊Order card

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/8-461x1024.jpg)

9.點擊Oreder my card

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/9-461x1024.jpg)

10.確認手機號以及郵寄地址，核對無誤後點擊Confirm address

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/10-461x1024.jpg)

11.設置一個四位數字的PIN

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/11-1-461x1024.jpg)

12.至此卡片就訂購完成啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/12-461x1024.jpg)

卡片也是支持Apple Pay/Google Pay的哦~
