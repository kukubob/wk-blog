今天給大家帶來一個eSIM，但是它只能用來上網，註冊激活就送1.5GB流量~

申請過程呢也很簡單，不需要實名認證，相對還是很友好的~

好了，話不多說，走你！

**申請過程**

1.打開App Store/Google Play，搜索下載Eskimo App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-49-31-581_com.android.vending-450x1024.jpg)

2.打開App，點擊下面的Next跳過

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-53-33-841_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

3.進到主頁後點擊右上角的Login or Signup

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-53-46-196_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

4.輸入你的手機號

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-53-54-777_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

5.輸入收到的認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-54-15-079_travel.eskimo.esim-edit-450x1024.jpg)

6.根據提示填寫個人訊息，然後在Referral code處填寫**ZESI19689**領取500MB流量

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-57-25-573_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

7.出現此提示就是領取成功了

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-58-41-702_travel.eskimo.esim-edit-450x1024.jpg)

8.註冊流程完後會進到這個界面這時點擊Redeem

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-59-14-810_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

9.輸入**GOODTOFLY**，領取1GB流量

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-59-29-762_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

10.點擊Redeem now即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-14-59-37-735_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)
![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-15-00-01-753_travel.eskimo.esim_-450x1024.jpg)

11.esim.me用戶可以聯繫客服獲取eSIM二維碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screenshot_2023-06-23-15-12-39-047_com.google.android.gm_-450x1024.jpg)

最後，你們應該知道我要幹啥了，！
