**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.northernrailway.co.uk/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.northernrailway.co.uk/tickets/smart)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-24-1024x510.png)

2.下拉點擊Request a Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-25-1024x416.png)

3.點擊Register，填寫個人信息後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-26.png)

4.填寫地址信息，然後點擊Order Smaertcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-27.png)

5.至此卡片就完成訂購啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-28.png)

6.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-29.png)
