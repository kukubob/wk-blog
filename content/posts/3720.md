**申請過程**

1.打開App Store/Google Play，搜索下載RedteaGo App

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-05-35-987_com.android.vending-450x1024.jpg)

2.打開App，點擊右下角的Me

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-06-39-098_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

3.點擊Sign up

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-06-42-171_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

4.輸入郵箱

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-06-46-235_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

5.創建一個密碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-06-58-731_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

6.輸入郵箱收到的6位數認證碼

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-07-11-217_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

7.註冊完成後點擊右下角的Me，然後點擊Promo Code

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-08-08-181_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

8.輸入**ZESI0001**，然後點擊Redeem

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-08-27-266_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

9.回到剛才的頁面會發現你收到了US$3.00餘額

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-09-05-087_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)

10.App主頁搜索China，購買1GB 31天數據包即可

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot_2024-02-16-17-09-37-154_com.redteamobile.redteago-450x1024.jpg)
