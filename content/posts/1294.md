**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.southwesternrailway.com/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://www.southwesternrailway.com/train-tickets/smart-ticketing/touch-smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-88-1024x425.png)

2.下拉點擊Get a smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-89-1024x639.png)

3.點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-90.png)

4.填寫姓名、手機號、郵箱地址，設置密碼後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-91-1024x653.png)

5.點擊Save

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-92.png)

6.選擇生日，輸入卡暱稱，然後點擊Continue

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-93.png)

7.至此卡片就完成訂購啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-94.png)
