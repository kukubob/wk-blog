**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://tfw.wales/)，找到[Smartcard界面](https://tfw.wales/ways-to-travel/rail/ticket-types/smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-104-1024x502.png)

2.點擊Get a Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-105.png)

3.切換到Register，輸入姓名、郵箱，設置密碼後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-106.png)

4.輸入收件地址，然後點擊Order Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-107.png)

5.至此卡片就訂購完成啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-109.png)

6.郵箱也會收到一封確認信哦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-110.png)
