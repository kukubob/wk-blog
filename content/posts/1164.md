**網上申請過程**

1.打開[官網](https://www.greateranglia.co.uk/)，找到[Smart界面](https://www.greateranglia.co.uk/smart)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-18-1024x616.png)

2.下拉找到[Order your FREE Smart Card](https://www.buytickets.greateranglia.co.uk/smartcard)

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-19-1024x593.png)

3.輸入姓名、郵箱，設置密碼後點擊Register

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-20.png)

4.輸入收件地址，然後點擊Order Smartcard

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-21.png)

5.至此卡片就訂購完成啦~

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/image-22.png)
